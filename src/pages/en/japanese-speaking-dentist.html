Why language matters in dentistry

Explaining a diagnosis, weighing up treatment options and asking questions about a procedure all require precise vocabulary. When the conversation happens in a second language, questions often go unasked — and the decision about one's own treatment suffers for it.

At Fukuoka Dental Clinic, care in Japanese covers every stage: the initial history, the presentation of the treatment plan, post-operative instructions and follow-up.
