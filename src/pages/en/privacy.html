Privacy Policy

This policy describes how Fukuoka Dental Clinic handles personal data, in accordance with Brazilian Law No. 13.709/2018 (LGPD, the General Data Protection Law).

What data we collect

Browsing data. When you visit the site, we collect technical information such as IP address, browser type, device, pages visited and time spent. This data is obtained through cookies and analytics tools, and serves to understand how the site is used and to improve it.

Contact data. When you get in touch by WhatsApp, telephone or email, we receive the data you provide: name, telephone number, email address and the content of your message.

Health data. Clinical information is collected only during in-person appointments, never through this website. The LGPD classifies health data as sensitive personal data, subject to heightened protection. It forms part of the dental record and is also governed by the rules of the Brazilian Federal Council of Dentistry.

What we use it for

  • Responding to enquiries and scheduling appointments
  • Providing the dental care you have engaged us for
  • Meeting legal and regulatory obligations, including retention of dental records
  • Measuring site performance and improving the browsing experience

Legal bases

Processing relies, as applicable, on the data subject's consent, on the performance of a contract, on compliance with a legal or regulatory obligation, and on the protection of health — the latter provided for in article 11, II, "f" of the LGPD for sensitive data processed by health professionals.

Who we share it with

We do not sell personal data. Sharing is limited to service providers necessary to our operation — such as hosting, communication and traffic analytics providers — and to authorities, where required by law or court order.

The analytics tools used on this site may transfer data outside Brazil, in accordance with the policies of the respective providers.

Cookies

Cookies are small files stored in your browser. We use cookies that are necessary for the site to work and analytics cookies, which depend on your consent. You can block or delete them at any time in your browser settings; the site will continue to work, but some measurements will no longer take place.

How long we keep it

Contact data is kept for as long as needed to deal with the enquiry. Dental records are retained for the period required by professional regulation. Browsing data follows the retention period configured in the analytics tools.

Your rights

The LGPD guarantees you the right, at any time and free of charge, to:

  • Confirm that your data is being processed
  • Access the data we hold about you
  • Correct incomplete, inaccurate or out-of-date data
  • Request the anonymisation, blocking or deletion of data that is unnecessary or processed unlawfully
  • Request portability to another provider
  • Withdraw your consent
  • Be informed about who we share your data with

How to exercise your rights
