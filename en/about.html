About

A clinic built on precision and listening

Fukuoka Dental Clinic was founded on the conviction that excellent dentistry requires two things in equal measure: technical rigour and time to understand each person.

Inspired by Japanese tradition, the clinic works to a standard in which meticulous attention to detail is not a differentiator but the baseline. It shows in the digital planning that precedes every procedure, in the preference for minimally invasive approaches, and in the way each stage is explained before it is carried out.

Mission

Dentistry has the power to transform lives

We believe dentistry has the power to transform lives.

Our mission is to help each patient reach their best version, promoting health, confidence and quality of life through dentistry of excellence, grounded in science, technology, ethics and genuinely human care.

At Fukuoka Dental Clinic, every smile is treated with precision, respect and dedication, because we believe that transforming a smile also transforms how a person lives, smiles and relates to the world.

How we work

Three stages, no surprises

  1. 01

    Assessment

    Clinical examination, imaging and digital scanning, with time set aside to understand what you expect from treatment.

  2. 02

    Personalised plan

    Options, stages and timelines presented before any decision. You choose with the information in hand.

  3. 03

    Treatment and follow-up

    Guided technology and periodic reviews, including after treatment is complete.
