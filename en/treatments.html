Treatments

Dental treatments on Avenida Paulista

Every procedure follows the same principle: preserve as much natural tooth structure as possible, and plan before intervening. Indication always depends on clinical examination.

Invisalign® clear aligners

A sequence of custom-made transparent trays moves the teeth in small, planned steps. Planning starts from an intraoral digital scan, which replaces conventional impressions and allows the predicted outcome to be simulated before treatment begins.

Aligners are removed for eating and brushing, and the recommended wear is 20 to 22 hours a day. Suitable for many cases of crowding, spacing and relapse after previous orthodontics; complex movements may respond more predictably to fixed appliances.

The active phase is always followed by retention, which preserves the position achieved. Retention is part of the treatment, not an optional extra.

Dental implants

An implant replaces the root, not the crown: a titanium post placed in the jawbone, onto which the prosthesis is fitted. Beyond aesthetics, it preserves the bone in the region, which is progressively resorbed once the root is lost.

Planning starts with a cone beam CT scan, which shows bone volume and density and the position of structures that must be preserved. Where the case allows, surgery is carried out with a printed surgical guide, which tends to shorten the procedure and reduce the opening required.

Osseointegration usually takes three to six months before the definitive prosthesis. In selected cases a temporary prosthesis can be fitted on the same day.

Teeth whitening

Whitening begins with a clinical examination. Untreated decay, leaking restorations, gum recession and pre-existing sensitivity all change the approach, and only show up on examination.

In-office whitening uses a higher concentration applied by the clinician in controlled sessions; supervised at-home whitening uses custom trays and a lower concentration over a set period. The two can be combined.

Transient sensitivity is the most common side effect and usually resolves within a few days. Restorations and crowns do not respond to the whitening agent and may need replacing afterwards to match the new shade.

Oral rehabilitation

For cases where loss of tooth structure affects chewing, speech or appearance across the whole mouth: multiple missing teeth, severe wear from bruxism, extensive fractures, or earlier restorations that no longer do their job.

Diagnosis brings together clinical examination, photographs, digital models and imaging, so the outcome can be designed before any intervention and the sequence between specialities defined. Active periodontal disease and sources of infection are treated first.

Temporary prostheses are not just a gap-filler: they let form and function be tested for a period before the definitive work is made.

Porcelain veneers

Thin ceramic laminates bonded to the visible surface of the tooth, correcting shape, colour, minor misalignment and wear, without changing the position of the teeth in the arch.

Our guiding principle is minimal preparation. Where the case allows, reduction is minimal or unnecessary; enamel removed does not grow back, and a case that needs little preparation today keeps other options open tomorrow.

The result is designed digitally beforehand, and in many cases can be trialled directly in the mouth with temporary material, so shape and proportion can be discussed before anything irreversible is done.
